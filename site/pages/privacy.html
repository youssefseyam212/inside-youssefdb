Inside youssefdb is a learning page that runs the youssefdb database in your browser. This page explains what happens to your data while you use it.

The code and data you type

Everything runs on your own device. The code you write, the documents you insert and the files you see in the Disk panel exist only in the memory of your browser tab. They are not sent to me or to any server, and they are gone when you reload or close the tab.

The Download data button creates a JSON file on your device. That file is made inside your browser and is not uploaded anywhere.

What I don’t collect

There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics and no advertising on this site. The page does not save anything in your browser’s storage.

Fonts from Google

The page loads its fonts (IBM Plex Sans and JetBrains Mono) from Google Fonts. To do that, your browser connects to Google’s servers, which can see your IP address and basic browser details. See Google’s privacy policy.
